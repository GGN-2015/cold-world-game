Cold World piano BGM performances

The nine MP3 performances in this directory are deterministic, sample-free
procedural piano renderings generated by tools/generate_music.py. To the extent
copyright or related rights apply to these project-generated performances,
they are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The underlying scores and MIDI encodings are marked Public Domain by the
Mutopia Project. Each track's source page, original MIDI URL, transcription
maintainer and source checksum are recorded in manifest.json and tools/music.
The recordings are not performances by the composers or the score maintainers.
No third-party performance recordings or soundfont samples are included.

See docs/development/music.md for the complete repertoire and playback mechanism.
